How it works in 1 minute

01

You provide the app link

One actions.json describes the URL, viewport, and steps. agent-browser navigates the actual app and captures real screens (shots + steps.json).

02

Animate on top

The fixed viewport becomes the coordinate space. The cursor targets controls, zoom highlights the action, and Kokoro narration is timed to match each step.

03

Renders to MP4

HyperFrames runs headless Chrome + FFmpeg and exports the walkthrough in 16:9 with a browser frame and the INEMA.CLUB CTA. No cloud, no API.
